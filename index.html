STANDARD TAKEOFF BRIEFING
▼
"Call out any malfunction or warning. When any malfunction occurs before V1, I will call 'STOP' or 'GO'. Before V1, I will reject the takeoff for engine failure, any warning, caution or condition that significantly affects the safety of the flight."
FIRST OFFICER SECTOR BRIEFING (by Captain)
▼
"I shall have control of the thrust levers for the takeoff. If I call 'STOP', I will take over control and reject the takeoff. If I call 'GO', you will continue the takeoff until I say, 'I HAVE CONTROL'."

"If you initiate a go-around, you will continue to fly until I say, 'I HAVE CONTROL'. Below minima, if I call 'I HAVE CONTROL, GO-AROUND', I will take over control and reject the landing."

  • No poor braking action reported

  • Anti-ice fluids on wings
  • Contaminated runway (RwyCC 4 or less)
  • Landing gear down ops
  • Anti-skid inop
  • Wet runway with rev inop
IFLD Calculation Required
  1. LDA < 2000 m
  2. Runway contaminated
  3. CDL / MEL items impacting landing performance
  4. NOTAMs affecting landing performance (e.g. displaced threshold, obstacles, runway exits)
  5. Required by abnormal procedures
  6. Any other condition that may adversely affect approach speed and/or deceleration capability (e.g. adverse weather)
